# References

Checked 2026-10-15 (from the proposal).

1. Eswaran, K. P., & Tarjan, R. E. (1976). "Augmentation problems." *SIAM Journal on Computing*, 5(4), 653–665. — Proves the cost-based problem is NP-complete (Theorem 7).
2. Khuller, S. (1995). "Approximation algorithms for finding highly connected subgraphs." University of Maryland technical report. — Spanning tree plus augmentation is within factor 3; also describes Khuller & Vishkin (1994, *JACM* 41(2), 214–235).
3. Borradaile, G., & Klein, P. (2015). "The two-edge connectivity survivable-network design problem in planar graphs." arXiv:1302.2184. — Background and telecom motivation.
4. Bang-Jensen, J. (2007). "First obligatory project in DM85, Spring 2007." University of Southern Denmark. — Defines the spanning-tree-then-augment idea and greedy cover rule.
5. Kruskal, J. B. (1956). "On the shortest spanning subtree of a graph and the traveling salesman problem." *Proceedings of the AMS*, 7(1), 48–50. doi:10.1090/S0002-9939-1956-0078686-7.
6. Bang-Jensen, J., Chiarandini, M., & Morling, P. (2010). "A computational investigation of heuristic algorithms for 2-edge-connectivity augmentation." *Networks*, 55(4), 299–325. doi:10.1002/net.20316.
